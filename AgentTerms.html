Agent Agreement

Last Updated: August 28, 2026  ·  Version 1.0
This Agreement applies specifically to individuals registering or operating as a CashWay Agent. It supplements, and does not replace, CashWay's Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy, both of which continue to apply to your account.

1. Purpose of This Agreement

By applying to become, or operating as, a CashWay Agent, you agree to the additional obligations set out below, in addition to CashWay's general Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

2. Agent Eligibility

To register as an Agent, you must be at least 18 years old, complete CashWay's identity verification process (including submission of a verification photograph), provide accurate registration information, and hold a valid Tanzanian phone number.

3. Agent Responsibilities

  • Maintain sufficient physical cash on hand to fulfill accepted delivery requests
  • Deliver the exact requested cash amount to the customer
  • Collect the full applicable payment (requested amount, service fee, and delivery fee) directly from the customer at the point of delivery
  • Follow CashWay's delivery, verification, and OTP confirmation procedures for every transaction
  • Protect customer information, including location and contact details, and use it solely to complete the assigned delivery
  • Act honestly, professionally, and in good faith in all dealings with customers

4. Fees and Settlement

For each completed delivery, CashWay's applicable share is calculated as a 3% service fee plus 20% of the TZS 1,000 delivery fee. This amount is recorded against your CashWay settlement balance and is not deducted electronically.

Your outstanding settlement balance may reach a maximum ceiling of TZS 5,000. Once this ceiling is reached, you will be prevented from accepting further delivery requests until your balance is cleared through CashWay's designated settlement process.

Changes to fees or the debt ceiling: CashWay will provide at least 14 days' advance notice via in-app notification and SMS before any change to the fee percentage, delivery fee share, or debt ceiling takes effect. Changes do not apply retroactively to already-completed deliveries.

5. Responsibility for Cash

As an Agent, you are personally responsible for the physical safekeeping, accuracy, and correct delivery of any cash you carry on CashWay's platform. You may be held responsible for losses arising from your own error, negligence, fraud, or failure to follow CashWay's procedures.

CashWay is not a bank, custodian, or insurer of cash carried by Agents, and does not guarantee reimbursement for cash lost, stolen, or misdelivered due to Agent error.

6. Delivery Confirmation

You must obtain the customer's delivery confirmation OTP only after the correct cash amount has actually been handed to the customer. Marking a delivery as complete before cash has been physically delivered, or without a valid OTP, is a violation of this Agreement and may result in suspension.

7. Prohibited Conduct

As an Agent, you must not: falsify or manipulate GPS or location data; deliver an incorrect cash amount; request or accept payment from a customer beyond the amount shown in the app; misappropriate customer funds; share your account, OTPs, or verification credentials with any other person; operate multiple Agent accounts; or engage in any fraudulent, unlawful, or abusive conduct on the platform.

8. Suspension and Termination of Agent Status

CashWay may suspend or terminate your Agent status where there is reasonable evidence of fraud, repeated customer complaints, failure to clear your settlement balance, violation of this Agreement, or unlawful conduct. Where appropriate, CashWay will provide notice or an opportunity to resolve the issue, except in cases of suspected fraud or a safety risk, where CashWay may act immediately.

Suspension or termination of your Agent status does not automatically affect your ability to use CashWay as a customer, unless the underlying conduct also violates CashWay's general Terms and Conditions.

9. Disputes Involving Agents

Disputes between an Agent and a customer, or between an Agent and CashWay regarding settlement balances, will be reviewed using available evidence, including OTP confirmation records, GPS/location data, order history, and account records, in accordance with the dispute process set out in CashWay's Terms and Conditions.

10. Relationship Between You and CashWay

Acting as a CashWay Agent does not create an employment relationship between you and CashWay. You operate as an independent participant on the platform, responsible for your own conduct, tax obligations, and compliance with applicable Tanzanian law in the course of your activity as an Agent.

11. Changes to This Agreement

CashWay may update this Agreement as the platform develops or as required by law or regulation. Material changes affecting fees, the debt ceiling, or Agent obligations require at least 14 days' notice and may require re-acceptance before you can continue accepting delivery requests. The version you accepted is recorded against your account.

12. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania, and is read together with CashWay's Terms and Conditions regarding governing law and dispute jurisdiction.
